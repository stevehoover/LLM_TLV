The goal is a clean two-part interface: input pipesignals assigned from SV signals at the top of the \TLV region, Verilog outputs assigned from pipesignals at the end, and no direct use of Verilog i/o signals inside logic expressions in between. FAIL if any logic expression between those two sections still references a *-prefixed Verilog signal directly, or if the input/output bridging sections themselves contain real logic rather than plain connections.
