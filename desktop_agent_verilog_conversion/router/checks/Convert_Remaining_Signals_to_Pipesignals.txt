The goal is that logic previously expressed on plain Verilog signals is now expressed on TLV pipesignals. FAIL if logic that could be converted remains on Verilog signals, and especially if assignments or expressions were merely relocated into \SV_plus blocks instead of being converted. \SV_plus is acceptable only for constructs TL-Verilog genuinely cannot express (such as module instantiation or memory arrays), not as a container for ordinary combinational or sequential assignments.
