User guidance for "Non-vector Signals", from diagnosing your failed attempts
and from this module's previously completed conversion (the lines below are
byte-exact from its verified checkpoint for this exact conversion).

WHY YOUR ATTEMPTS FAILED: you wrote declaration lines like
   **logic [8:0] $imm19_b12_b20;
at TLV scope. That is not TL-Verilog: pipesignals are NEVER declared, they
exist by being assigned. SandPiper rejects those lines with
RECOV_ERROR (PARSE-ASSIGN) "couldn't find '='", which is exactly what your
FEV runs showed. Do not invent declaration syntax; there is none to write.

VERIFIED PATTERN. To convert a non-vector (or vector) reg to a pipesignal,
replace its always-block update with ONE next-value assignment at TLV scope
using the <<1 retiming form, and delete the reg declaration. Byte-exact lines
from the previous conversion of this module (W=4 arm registers):

         <<1$ii7 = *i_cnt_en ? $signbit : *i_wb_en ? *i_wb_rdt[7] : $ii7;
         <<1$ii9 = *i_cnt_en ? $ii26 : *i_wb_en ? *i_wb_rdt[9] : $ii9;
         <<1$ii19 = *i_cnt_en ? (*i_ctrl[3] ? $signbit : $ii23) : *i_wb_en ? *i_wb_rdt[19] : $ii19;

Reading the rules off those lines:
  - LHS `<<1$name` means "the value of $name next cycle": this IS the flop.
  - RHS: module inputs are read as `*i_name`; other ALREADY-CONVERTED signals
    as `$name`; NOT-yet-converted regs as `*name`; the hold term is `$name`
    (current value), which is what makes it a self-holding register.
  - No declaration line anywhere. The assignment brings the signal into being.
  - Place each assignment at TLV scope inside the SAME m5 arm that owned the
    reg, before that arm's \SV_plus block.

In the same attempt, for each converted register:
  - Delete its `reg` declaration and its assignment from the always block
    inside \SV_plus.
  - Change remaining reads of it inside \SV_plus to `$name` (reads of a
    pipesignal from \SV_plus are legal, e.g. `assign o_csr_imm = {$ii18,
    $ii17, $ii16, $ii15};` is from the verified run).
  - Add to fev.eqy's [match serv_immdec] section:
        gold-match <reg_name> |default<>0$<name>
    (gold side: the reg's name as it exists in feved.sv; gate side: the TLV
    pipesignal reference, which fev.sh maps to the generated Verilog name).
  - Update the same signal's gate side in fev_full.eqy,
    fev_full_W_1_SHARED_0.eqy and fev_full_W_4.eqy to |default<>0$<name>,
    keeping their gold side (gen_immdec_w_eq_*.<reg_name>) unchanged.
