User guidance for "Convert Remaining Signals to Pipesignals", from diagnosing
the failed attempts of both A/B runs on this exact task.

The conversion mechanics are the same as the previous task: replace each reg's
always-block update with ONE next-value assignment at TLV scope in the same m5
arm, `<<1$name = ...` on the left, inputs as `*i_name`, converted signals as
`$name`, unconverted regs as `*name`, hold term `$name`. No declaration lines,
pipesignals exist by being assigned. Convert a FEW signals per pass.

Both runs failed on this task for two DIFFERENT bookkeeping mistakes. Avoid
both:

MISTAKE 1 (syntax of match lines). One run wrote fev.eqy gate references as
   gold-match imm31 |default$imm31@0
and the harness's pipesignal mapper rejected the whole file ("Failed to map
TLV pipesignals to Verilog in fev.eqy"). The `@0` suffix is not part of a
pipesignal reference. The correct, previously verified form uses the <>0
stage alignment:
   gold-match imm31 |default<>0$imm31
One line per converted register in fev.eqy (gold side: the reg name as it
exists in feved.sv). Update the same signals' gate side in fev_full.eqy,
fev_full_W_1_SHARED_0.eqy and fev_full_W_4.eqy to |default<>0$name, keeping
their gold side (gen_immdec_w_eq_*.name) unchanged.

MISTAKE 2 (leftover bare reads). The other run deleted a reg's declaration
and update but left a read of the bare name somewhere in the remaining SV
(another m5 arm, an assign, a concatenation). With `default_nettype none`
that becomes an implicitly declared wire and yosys fails with
   ERROR: Identifier `\imm31' is implicitly declared
After converting a register, EVERY remaining reference to it in EVERY m5 arm
must become `$name` (reads of pipesignals from \SV_plus are legal). Before
submitting, search the whole file for the bare name to confirm none is left.

Remember the m5 arms are alternative texts of the SAME module: a register you
convert exists in more than one arm's code, and every arm that mentions it
must be updated in the same attempt.
