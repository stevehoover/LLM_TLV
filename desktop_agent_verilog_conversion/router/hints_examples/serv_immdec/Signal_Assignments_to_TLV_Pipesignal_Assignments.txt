User guidance for "Signal Assignments to TLV Pipesignal Assignments", from
diagnosing your failed attempts and from this module's previously completed
conversion (the example below is its verified byte-exact step for this task).

WHY YOUR ATTEMPTS FAILED: you wrote pipesignal assignments like
   $signbit = *imm31 & ! *i_csr_imm_en;
INSIDE the \SV_plus block. Inside \SV_plus, a `$name` is only a USE of the
pipesignal, never an assignment, so SandPiper reports
"LOGIC_ERROR (UNASSIGNED-SIG): Signal |default$... is used but never
assigned." Pipesignal assignments must sit in TLV context, at the SAME
indentation level as `\SV_plus` itself (directly under @0), never inside it.

VERIFIED PATTERN from the previous conversion of this module, same structure
as your file. Because signbit has a different definition per m5 arm, the
assignment lives in its own small m5_if_eq_block AT TLV SCOPE, placed before
the big \SV_plus block:

         // Pipesignal assignments
         m5_if_eq_block(m5_cond_w_1, 1, ['
         $signbit = *imm31 & ! *i_csr_imm_en;
         '], ['
         $signbit = *ii31 & ! *i_csr_imm_en;
         '])

(use YOUR current register names on the right-hand side; `*name` reads a
module-level Verilog signal from TLV context). Then, in the SAME attempt:
  - DELETE the `wire signbit = ...;` declaration from BOTH m5 arms inside
    \SV_plus.
  - Replace every use of `signbit` inside \SV_plus with `$signbit` (reading
    a TLV pipesignal from \SV_plus is legal, assigning is not).

The next step it did (also verified) was the clock alias, same shape:
         // Simple signal assignment converted to TL-Verilog pipesignal
         $clk = *i_clk;
with the `wire clk = i_clk;` line deleted and always blocks becoming
`always \@(posedge $clk)` (note the escaped \@ inside \SV_plus).

RULES:
- Convert ONE signal per fev.sh pass. This task is iterative; small steps
  keep FEV failures attributable.
- Convert simple fixed-width internal signals (signbit, clk alias). Leave
  the W-parameterized outputs (o_csr_imm, o_imm) and the shift registers
  alone; they belong to later tasks.
- Do not resubmit an unchanged file; the reviewer compares before and after.
- If incremental FEV later fails UNKNOWN on partitions after a conversion,
  check whether the converted signal needs a gold-match line in fev.eqy
  (gold side: its old plain name in feved.sv; gate side may use the TLV
  pipesignal reference).
