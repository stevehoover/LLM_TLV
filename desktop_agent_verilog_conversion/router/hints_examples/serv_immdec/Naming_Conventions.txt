User guidance for "Naming Conventions", from diagnosing your 12 failed FEV runs
and from this module's previously completed conversion (its history checkpoint
for this exact task is the source of the verified example below).

WHY YOUR ATTEMPTS FAILED: you renamed signals in wip.tlv but wrote no match
lines into fev.eqy (you only touched fev_full*.eqy). Incremental FEV compares
feved.sv (old names) against wip.sv (new names). EQY cuts the design at
matched registers; a renamed register with no match line does not get cut, so
the whole self-referencing shift register lands inside one partition and
k-induction returns UNKNOWN (you saw basecase pass and induction fail on
serv_immdec.o_rs2_addr.4 and o_rd_addr.4). The fix is bookkeeping, not logic.

RULE: for EVERY signal you rename, in the same attempt:

1. Add to the [match serv_immdec] section of fev.eqy:
     gold-match <old_name> <new_name>
   The gold side is the name as it exists in feved.sv (the state before your
   edit; plain flat names like imm24_20, i31). The gate side is your new name.

2. Update the GATE side of that signal's existing gold-match lines in
   fev_full.eqy, fev_full_W_1_SHARED_0.eqy and fev_full_W_4.eqy. Their gold
   side keeps the prepared.sv scoped names (gen_immdec_w_eq_1.*,
   gen_immdec_w_eq_4.*) and must not change.

VERIFIED EXAMPLE from the previous conversion of this module: it renamed the
W=4 arm registers i31..i7 to ii31..ii7 (and i7_2, i20_2 to ii7_b2 style
names). After the task, its fev_full_W_4.eqy match section read:
     gold-match gen_immdec_w_eq_4.i31 ii31
     gold-match gen_immdec_w_eq_4.i30 ii30
     ... (one line per renamed register, old prepared name -> new name)
and during the task its fev.eqy carried the corresponding
     gold-match i31 ii31
style lines, which the harness consumed after the pass.

REMINDERS:
- Rename only. Do not restructure logic, do not change strategies, do not
  touch [collect] or [partition] sections.
- The harness EMPTIES fev.eqy's match section after every successful fev.sh
  run. If your first attempt fails and you edit further, verify your rename
  lines are still present in fev.eqy before the next run.
- If you rename nothing in a config-specific arm, its fev_full file needs no
  change.
